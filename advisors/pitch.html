Working together

Your data never leaves your systems.

01

We scope it. A short call defines exactly what gets covered: a watchlist, a set of filings, one real question. All of it agreed before anything starts.

02

It runs on your side. Against your own data feeds, on your systems or a private environment you control. Your research stays yours.

03

You get a written report. What it found, how confident it is, why, and the sources it read. A section is left blank on purpose for your own judgment before anything goes further.

04

NDA first, always. Signed before we see anything specific about your watchlist or your names, not after. Standard for every engagement, no exceptions.

05

You decide what's next. Act on it, ignore it, or come back with a follow-up question. The report is yours either way. There's no obligation to continue past what we scoped.
