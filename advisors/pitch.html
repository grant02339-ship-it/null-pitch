How the AI part actually works

Two layers. Only one of them is AI.

Here's exactly what "powered by AI" means: the AI does the reading and writing. The checking is just math.

Layer 1: Checking

Plain math, not AI

Scoring every past read against the real price move that followed, using free market data. Split out by how confident the call said it was, so you can see directly whether higher stated confidence has actually meant more often right. Same input, same answer, every time. No model makes this call.

Layer 2: Research

Where the AI actually works

Claude reads the filings and the news, searches systematically rather than stopping at the first plausible result, and turns what it finds into a structured read: a sentiment, a confidence level, and the actual sources it used. It's fast. It can also sound confident while it's wrong, which is exactly why it never skips the layer above.
