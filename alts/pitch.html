01
The problem

A 200-page document has a real amount of reading in it

What matters most, exit terms and every fee layer, is rarely summarized up front. Finding it means reading the whole document.

The old way
An afternoon per prospectus, read by hand
What changes
A few minutes for the first pass, then your own review
02
What it reads

One tool, built for one document type

Finds the fund's actual SEC filing: Form N-2 for interval funds and non-traded BDCs, Form S-11 or 10-K for non-traded REITs. Reads the repurchase terms, every fee layer, and fund-specific risks, not boilerplate.

  • Every number comes from the filing, with a citation
  • Flags terms easy to miss on a skim
  • Reads the primary document itself, every time
03
How it's checked

Every read is logged and checked against what the fund does next

These funds have no market price to check against. Instead: the fund's published NAV, and whether its own repurchase terms held or were suspended. Each read is logged: the terms, the fee layers, the concern level.

Once there's enough graded history, Claude reviews its own track record for patterns, citing a real sample size, never a trading rule.
